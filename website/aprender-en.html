1. Open

In the APPS column, OPEN starts the instrument installed on the computer. Its sound no longer goes straight to the speakers: it arrives here, on its own strip in OUTPUTS AND STEMS. When Maestruc closes, everything returns to the speakers and the apps stay open. An app opened outside Maestruc shows as OUTSIDE; CONNECT brings it in. An instrument that is not installed shows as NOT INSTALLED: HOW TO INSTALL leads to its official page. Maestruc never downloads or installs anything.

2. Mix

Each strip has level (drag; double-click = 0 dB; Shift = fine; arrow keys), mute (M), solo (S) and where it goes: mix, mix + phones, phones or none. Master and Phones choose the output device (for example, the audio interface for the speakers and the computer's card for the headphones).

3. Mixers

In MIXERS, the mixer inside each app that has one, with its own controls; what changes in the app comes back here. In Navalha 2, LEVEL, MUTE and SOLO belong only to whoever plays.

4. Influence

In the matrix, the row is who influences and the column who is influenced; the last column is the Master, the whole mix. Pick a cell, what changes (level or filter), the amount and the smoothing, then + NEW INFLUENCE. Example: Batruc over Aquorbium, level −0.6: when Batruc plays, Aquorbium makes room; when it falls silent, it comes back over the smoothing time. This happens in the sound, here in Maestruc. Asking the app to change its own music (in the music) does not exist yet.

5. Conduct

In CONDUCTING, each app has a seed and a piece. When opened by Maestruc, the app starts with them: seed in Batruc, Rasgo Modular, Aquorbium, Asterismus, Antitotem and Synth Studio; piece in Batruc; project in Navalha 2. With the app open and the channel connected, SEND SEED and OPEN PIECE change the seed or the piece right away in the apps that accept them; PLAY ALL and STOP reach everyone that accepts them.

6. Record

RECORD writes each ticked strip and the Master as 24-bit WAV, one folder per take, with a log.json of what happened. When it stops, a delivery version for streaming is written next to the Master (−14 LUFS, true peak up to −1 dBTP); the raw recording does not change. If the disk lags, the music does not stop: what does not fit is counted. Phones are not recorded.

7. Machine

The bar shows how many cores the apps use and, lighter, the whole system; the mark is the 70% limit. The ECONOMY and MINIMUM budgets ask the apps that accept them to spend less, without changing the composition.

8. Versions

If you allow it, once a day Maestruc checks online whether each installed instrument has a new version; the card shows NEW VERSION, which opens the official page. You can turn this on or off in ABOUT.
