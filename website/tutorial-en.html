Tutorial

A first session, from the start to a recorded take. Names in capitals are the ones the interface shows.

1. The instruments

Maestruc opens the RASGO instruments already on the computer; it installs none itself.

  • Install at least two instruments, each from its own site (Batruc and Aquorbium make a good first pair).
  • In the APPS column, a missing instrument shows as NOT INSTALLED; HOW TO INSTALL opens its official page.

2. Open

In APPS, OPEN Batruc and Aquorbium.

  • Each opens in its own window, and its sound now reaches Maestruc, on a strip in OUTPUTS AND STEMS.
  • An instrument that was already open shows as OUTSIDE; CONNECT brings it into Maestruc.
  • When Maestruc closes, the sound returns to the speakers and the instruments stay open.

3. Mix

Each strip has level, mute (M), solo (S) and destination.

  • Drag the level; double-click returns to 0 dB; Shift gives fine control.
  • The destination chooses where the strip goes: mix, mix + phones, phones or none.
  • Master and Phones choose the output device: for example, the audio interface for the speakers and the computer's card for the headphones.

4. An influence

In the matrix, the row is who influences and the column who is influenced.

  • Pick the Batruc → Aquorbium cell, what changes (level), the amount (−0.6) and the smoothing.
  • + NEW INFLUENCE: when Batruc plays, Aquorbium makes room; when Batruc falls silent, Aquorbium comes back.
  • The last column is the Master, the whole mix.

5. Conduct

In CONDUCTING, each instrument has a seed and a piece.

  • When opened by Maestruc, the instrument starts with them.
  • With the instrument open, SEND SEED changes the seed right away, in the instruments that accept it.
  • PLAY ALL and STOP reach everyone that accepts them.

6. Record

Tick the strips you want and click RECORD; click again to stop.

  • Each take becomes a folder: Music/RASGO/Maestruc/recordings/<day>/take-01-<time>/.
  • Inside: master.wav, one strip per instrument (24-bit WAV), log.json with what happened and master-streaming.wav, the delivery version for streaming.
  • The … button next to the folder picks another place. Headphones are not recorded.

Core rule

Influence is not control. Maestruc asks; each instrument interprets in its own way and keeps playing on its own if Maestruc closes.
